COMPANY: TechNova Solutions
DEPARTMENT: Information Technology
DOCUMENT: IT Support Policy

1. Password Reset

Employees who forget their company
password must contact the IT helpdesk.

2. Laptop Replacement

Company laptops are replaced after
4 years of usage, subject to approval
from the IT department.

3. Technical Support

The IT helpdesk operates from
9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday.

4. Security

Employees must not share company
credentials with other employees.
