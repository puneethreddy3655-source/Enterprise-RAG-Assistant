COMPANY: TechNova Solutions
DEPARTMENT: Finance
DOCUMENT: Employee Travel Reimbursement Policy

1. Travel Reimbursement

Employees can claim reimbursement
for approved business travel expenses.

2. Hotel Accommodation

Hotel expenses must be supported
by original invoices.

3. Meal Allowance

Employees are eligible for a meal
allowance of Rs. 800 per day
during approved business travel.

4. Claim Submission

Travel reimbursement claims must
be submitted within 15 days
of completing the business trip.
