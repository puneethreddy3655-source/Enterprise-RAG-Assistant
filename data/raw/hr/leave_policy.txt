COMPANY: TechNova Solutions
DEPARTMENT: Human Resources
DOCUMENT: Employee Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave

All permanent employees are entitled to
24 days of annual paid leave per year.

2. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of
paid sick leave annually.

3. Casual Leave

Employees can take 6 days of casual leave
per calendar year.

4. Leave Approval

Employees must submit leave requests
through the HR portal.

Annual leave requests exceeding 5
consecutive working days require
approval from the department manager.

5. New Employees

Employees become eligible for annual
leave after completing 30 days of service.
